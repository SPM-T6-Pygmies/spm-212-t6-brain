4

Tentative Venue Holds

Expire

Venue Staff have explained that they sometimes temporarily hold a

venue while an Event Coordinator is finalising event arrangements.

These tentative holds should not reserve a venue indefinitely. Each hold

must therefore have an expiry date/time. If the required action is not

completed before the deadline, the hold should expire and the venue

should become available for other requests. The Event Coordinator

should be informed before or when the hold expires, and an expired

hold should not be treated as a confirmed booking.

5

Addition of Event

Coordinator Lead Role

ConnectSphere has introduced a new internal role, the Event

Coordinator Lead, who is responsible for overseeing incoming event

requests and assigning them to individual Event Coordinators. Newly

submitted event requests should no longer be assigned directly to an

Event Coordinator. Instead, they first enter an unassigned queue that

can be viewed by the Event Coordinator Lead. The Lead can review basic

event information, assign a suitable Event Coordinator, and reassign

events where necessary. Event Coordinators should only be able to

manage events assigned to them, while the Event Coordinator Lead

should be able to view all coordinator assignments and active events

under their supervision. Relevant users should be notified when

assignments or reassignments occur.

6

Operational Feasibility

Check

ConnectSphere has introduced a new internal role, the Safety Officer,

who is responsible for reviewing the operational safety of planned

events. After the venue and technical arrangements have been

confirmed, the Safety Officer must conduct an Operational Safety Check

before the event can proceed to preparation. The review considers

factors such as expected attendance, venue capacity and layout,

emergency access, accessibility requirements, equipment placement,

crowd movement, and any known venue restrictions. The Safety Officer

may approve the event, reject the safety arrangement, or request

changes. If changes are required, the event returns to the relevant

earlier planning stage and affected venue or technical arrangements

may need to be reviewed again. The event cannot proceed to

preparation until the Safety Officer has approved it. You can assume that

the Safety Officer will always give a response (approve/reject/request).
